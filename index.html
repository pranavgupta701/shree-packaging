What Makes Shree Packaging Special

Consistent quality, strict lab testing, and prompt delivery make us the trusted partner for top brands.

100% Customizable
Dimensions, ply strength, burst factor, paper GSM, flute type, and surface graphics are manufactured precisely to your specification.
In-House Testing Laboratory
Every batch is tested in our quality lab for bursting strength, compression, moisture percentage, Cobb sizing, and ply adhesion.
ISO 9001:2015 Certified
Standardized manufacturing processes and thorough quality audits guarantee dependable box strength and zero defect rates.
Rapid High-Volume Delivery
Continuous multi-deck corrugator and automatic flexo printer slotters enable fast lead times for bulk industrial orders.
Sustainable & Recyclable
All our corrugated products are 100% recyclable, biodegradable, and crafted from responsibly sourced kraft pulp.
