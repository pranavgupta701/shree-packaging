Who We Are

A Total Packaging Solution Provider

Established with a vision to deliver reliable packaging solutions, SHREE PACKAGING is one of the leading manufacturers, designers and distributors of corrugated boxes, cardboard boxes, and honeycomb boxes for domestic as well as international markets.

Equipped with modern manufacturing facilities and advanced technologies, we offer customized and innovative packaging products designed to fulfill diverse packaging requirements. With a strong focus on quality and customer satisfaction, SHREE PACKAGING follows efficient quality management practices to ensure consistent and dependable packaging solutions.

As a complete packaging solution provider, we specialize in the designing, printing, manufacturing and distribution of a wide range of packaging materials. Serving various industries and business sectors, we are committed to providing the right packaging solutions that meet the specific requirements, expectations and needs of our valued customers.

Quality Assurance

In-House Testing Laboratory

Our in-house laboratory ensures that every box meets rigorous burst index, crush resistance, and environmental standards.

Bursting Strength Tester
Measures maximum hydraulic pressure the corrugated board can withstand before bursting.
Digital Moisture Meter
Ensures paper reel and sheet moisture levels remain strictly within optimal curing limits.
Precision GSM Scale
Verifies grammage per square meter across liner boards and fluting mediums for exact weight specs.
Cobb Sizing Tester
Calculates water absorbency rate on paper surfaces to evaluate moisture-barrier integrity.
