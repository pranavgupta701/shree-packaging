Lab Facility

Key In-House Testing Facilities

Our in-house laboratory conducts comprehensive scientific testing of packaging materials and finished products.

Bursting Strength Test
Pneumatic testing confirms the burst index and strength under severe hydraulic pressure.
Moisture Content Test
Electronic moisture sensing ensures paper fibers maintain correct humidity for flute stiffness.
Paper GSM Testing
Precision balance verifies exact grammage per square meter on virgin and semi-kraft liners.
Cobb Water Sizing Test
Evaluates surface water absorption over specified time intervals for weather resistance.

Zero Monsoon Box Collapse Guarantee

Humidity-induced carton softening is a major risk during Indian monsoons. Every production dispatch from our plant is certified with an in-house Certificate of Analysis (COA) verifying tested moisture percentage (<9%), Cobb 60 water absorption, and bursting strength.
